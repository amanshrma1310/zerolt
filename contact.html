Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How fast can Zerolt launch a Webflow + GoHighLevel setup?

Our standard turnaround for a high-converting 5 to 8 page Webflow website with a fully wired GoHighLevel opportunity pipeline and automated SMS/email booking follow-up is typically 2 to 3 weeks.

Why do you recommend n8n over Zapier or Make?

While we support all platforms, self-hosted n8n allows high-volume lead processing without paying hundreds of dollars in monthly "task/operation" overages. You retain 100% control of your customer data and can run complex loops and AI agents natively.

How does LeadSpark integrate with our existing website?

LeadSpark requires adding a single lightweight JavaScript snippet (less than 12KB) to your header. It immediately starts monitoring inbound traffic, enrichments, and dispatches data straight into your CRM or webhook endpoints.

Do you provide ongoing support after deployment?

Yes! We offer monthly retainer packages covering continuous speed audits, new funnel split-testing, n8n server maintenance, GHL snapshot updates, and priority Slack channel support.
